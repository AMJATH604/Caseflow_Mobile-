WHY CASEFLOW?

The Problem We Solve

😰

The Problem

Indian advocates manage hundreds of active cases across multiple courts. Tracking hearing dates, managing documents, coordinating with junior lawyers, and maintaining client payment records is done manually through paper diaries.

  • ✗ Missed court hearings
  • ✗ Lost case documents
  • ✗ Revenue leakage from unpaid fees
  • ✗ No team coordination
🚀

Our Solution

CaseFlow provides a unified digital workspace that automates the entire legal workflow — from case filing to judgment — with real-time collaboration and automated reminders.

  • ✓ Automated hearing reminders
  • ✓ Cloud document vault
  • ✓ Fee tracking & payment ledger
  • ✓ Firm-level collaboration
