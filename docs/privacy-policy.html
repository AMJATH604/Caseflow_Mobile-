🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 8, 2026

CaseFlow ("we", "our", "us") is a legal practice management application designed for Indian advocates and citizens. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Summary: We collect only the data necessary to provide legal practice management services. Your data is encrypted, stored securely, and never sold to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Email address and password (for authentication)
  • Full name, mobile number, and bar registration number (for advocate profiles)
  • State and district (for legal jurisdiction context)
  • Profile photo (optional)

Case & Legal Data:

  • Case details (titles, court names, CNR numbers, hearing dates)
  • Client information (names, contact details)
  • Payment records (amounts, types — no credit card numbers are stored)
  • Evidence photos and documents uploaded by you
  • Legal research queries submitted to the AI assistant

Device & Usage Data:

  • Device type, operating system version (for crash reporting via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • FCM push notification tokens (for delivering hearing reminders and messages)
  • GPS coordinates (only when you explicitly use the Commission Field Kit feature)
  • Basic usage analytics (screens visited, feature usage — via Firebase Analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: Managing cases, hearings, payments, and client records
  • Notifications: Sending hearing reminders, chat messages, and caveat expiry alerts
  • AI Assistant: Processing your legal research queries through Google Gemini AI (your queries are not used to train AI models)
  • Firm Collaboration: Enabling shared data visibility within your law firm
  • Crash Reporting: Identifying and fixing app bugs via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Security: Audit logging for data integrity and unauthorized access detection

3. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL database) with Row Level Security (RLS) enforced on every table
  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption at all times
  • API keys for third-party services (Gemini AI) are stored as server-side secrets and never reach your device
  • Evidence photos include SHA-256 hashing for integrity verification
  • Authentication is handled via Supabase Auth with PKCE flow and secure JWT tokens
  • Sensitive case data is cached locally using encrypted SQLite for offline access

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data to any third party.

We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Firm Members: If you join a law firm in CaseFlow, firm members can see shared case data (controlled by you)
  • AI Processing: Legal queries are sent to Google Gemini API for processing. Google's AI usage policies apply.
  • Push Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging is used to deliver notifications
  • Legal Requirement: We may disclose data if required by law or court order

5. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data within the app
  • Export: You can export your case data as PDF or structured format
  • Deletion: You can delete individual records. For complete account deletion, contact us at the email below
  • Correction: You can update your profile and case information at any time
  • Portability: You can export and take your data with you

6. Data Retention

  • Active case data is retained as long as your account exists
  • Deleted records are soft-deleted (retained with a deleted_at timestamp for legal audit compliance) for 90 days, then permanently removed
  • Audit logs are retained for 1 year for legal compliance
  • Offline cache data is cleared on logout

7. Children's Privacy

CaseFlow is designed for legal professionals (advocates) and adult citizens. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

8. Third-Party Services

CaseFlow uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — Database, authentication, and file storage
  • Firebase — Push notifications, crash reporting, analytics
  • Google Gemini AI — AI-powered legal research

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email. Your continued use of CaseFlow after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
